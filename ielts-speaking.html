Before you start

How the test works

Treat it like the real thing. The examiner will not help, correct or comment on your answers during the test.

  • Speak naturally and in fullAnswer each question with developed answers, not single words. The test continues after you finish speaking.
  • Parts are timedPart 2 gives you one minute to prepare, then up to two minutes to speak. The examiner will stop you at two minutes.
  • Find a quiet placeUse headphones if you can. Background noise reduces the accuracy of speech recognition and of your results.
  • Your privacyNIRVO does not record or store your audio. Your browser's speech recognition may process it. Your transcript is used only to prepare your feedback.
  • Pronunciation is an estimateIt is based on browser speech-recognition signals, not on phoneme-level analysis, and is labelled as an estimate in your results.
